Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Who operates Elusive Hue

Elusive Hue is operated by Himanshu Rawat. For support, privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email himanshurawathiki@gmail.com.

Information used for your account

When you create an account with email, we use your email address and sign-in credentials to authenticate you and send account-confirmation or password-reset emails.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your account identifier, email address, name and profile-picture information with our authentication service. We use this information to create and authenticate your Elusive Hue account. Google sign-in requests only basic identity information, not access to Gmail, Google Drive or your other Google files.

You can choose a username and add a display name and bio. These profile fields are stored with your account. Signing in does not automatically publish artwork or create a public profile page.

Services that process information

We use Supabase for account authentication and account/profile storage, Cloudflare to host the website, and Google's email service to send account emails. These services process information needed to provide their parts of Elusive Hue. Choosing Google sign-in also involves Google's authentication service.

Information in your browser

Your browser stores sign-in session information so you can remain signed in. You can sign out through your account page. Signing out does not delete your account.

Editing and deleting your information

You can edit your username, display name and bio from your account page. Account and profile information remains stored while your account exists.

There is not yet a self-service account-deletion button. To request deletion, email himanshurawathiki@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We will check that the request is from the account holder before deleting the account and its saved profile information.

You can separately remove Elusive Hue's Google connection in your Google Account settings. Disconnecting Google stops that connection; it does not by itself delete your Elusive Hue account or profile. You do not need to give us your Google password to make a deletion request.

Changes

This policy describes the current release. We will update it before introducing new uses of account information or new marketplace features that change these practices.
